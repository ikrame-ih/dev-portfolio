01. the letter

Hello team,

I’m Ikrame. I started in operations, close to people, teams, and work that had to land every day. That’s where I learned to watch the details: the missing information, the steps that get repeated, the mess that grows because nobody had time to sort it.

When I found development, I knew I wanted the other side of that: the tools, the processes, and the systems that make the work a bit clearer for whoever comes next. It wasn’t an overnight switch. I learned, I built projects, I broke things and put them back together until programming stopped being something I studied and became how I solve problems.

Today I work mostly on backends. I like to understand before I build. What the person using the system needs, what can go wrong, and which decision should stay human. I use AI when it actually helps, but I still want the code to stay readable, the data to be validated, and someone to be able to review what happened.

I’m fairly meticulous about my work. I like leaving things thought through, documented, and tested. I also like keeping up. This academic year I’m starting the vocational master’s (máster de FP) in AI and big data, fully online, while I prepare GCP and AWS cloud certifications.

My projects tell the technical side. This letter tells the other: I care about useful software, with attention to the detail and to the person who will use it.

Best,
